# Artwork provenance

These packs contain native-resolution sprite crops derived from Nintendo's
Super Mario World. They are not represented as original artwork or as public
domain assets. No Nintendo affiliation or endorsement is claimed. Attribution
and this repository's availability do not grant redistribution or commercial
reuse rights to the underlying artwork. No artwork license is granted here.

Source reference sheets from The Spriters Resource:

- [Aquatic Enemies](https://www.spriters-resource.com/snes/smarioworld/asset/143231/)
- [Dry Bones & Boo Variants](https://www.spriters-resource.com/snes/smarioworld/asset/143232/)
- [Objects (Animated)](https://www.spriters-resource.com/snes/smarioworld/asset/144754/)

The sheets credit “Barack Obama” for ripping/recolouring Nintendo game artwork.
The pack conversion uses exact native crops, preserves their palettes, and turns
the sheet background key into transparency. Scene backgrounds, clock digits,
positioning, and animation behavior are rendered by the compatible firmware.
