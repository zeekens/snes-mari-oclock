# Artwork and adaptation credits

Game artwork: Square / Square Enix. Source sheets: The Spriters Resource.
Character sheets credit Tonberry2k / Unknown; consult the linked sheets for their full original credits.
This fan-made collection does not grant rights to third-party artwork.

Backgrounds and animation choreography are code-composed adaptations. Character poses use source pixels; vehicles, monsters and effects may be reduced with nearest-neighbour sampling. This is not a reproduction of canonical cutscenes. The display time is baked in as 12:34.
