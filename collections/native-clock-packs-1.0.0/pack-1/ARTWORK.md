# Artwork provenance

Game-derived artwork belongs to its respective owners, including Nintendo,
Square / Square Enix, Rare, Capcom and Konami. This fan-made collection grants
no rights to the underlying artwork and claims no affiliation or endorsement.
Sprite pixels and adapted scenery come from the gallery's source references
below. Original source sheets are not included. FFVI character sheets credit
Tonberry2k / Unknown; consult the original sheets for ripper credits.

Native SNTL scenes render the live clock. GIFs are previews with fixed 12:34.
Backgrounds, effects and choreography include 64×64 adaptations.
